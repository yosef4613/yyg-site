Which pages a business website usually has

  • Home: who you are, who you serve, and what to do next.
  • Services: a page for each main service, so Google gets to know each one.
  • Work or projects: proof that it works.
  • About: the face behind the business.
  • FAQ: saves repeat calls, and helps you show up on Google for questions people search.
  • Contact: phone, a short form, WhatsApp, and a map if people come to you.

Up to 6 pages are included, and each extra page is ₪350, so you can plan exactly how many you need. The price calculator suggests a list of pages by business type.

What an admin panel is, and what you update in it

An admin panel is a simple screen where you update whatever changes, without touching the design: prices, hours, promotions, photos, new projects or recipes. At HaKef SheBaAfiya, for example, the owner adds a new recipe on her own, and it appears on the site in the same design as everything else. I set up in advance what you update yourself and what stays fixed and protected, so the site can't be broken by accident.
