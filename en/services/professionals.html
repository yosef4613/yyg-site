Websites for trades and service providers

A website that makes your phone ring: anyone who finds you on Google understands right away what you do, where, and why to choose you, and calls with one tap.

Who it's for

  • Tradespeople in the field: electricians, plumbers, technicians and renovators.
  • Consultants and service providers: accountants, mortgage advisers, lawyers and coaches.
  • Therapists and private tutors who see clients by appointment.

What a tradesperson's website needs

  • A call button that's always visible on a phone, with WhatsApp next to it. Most people looking for a tradesperson search on their phone, and don't want to hunt for a way to get in touch.
  • A clear service area: which cities and neighborhoods you cover, so the right people reach out.
  • Services and prices: a clear list, and a starting price if possible. It filters out irrelevant inquiries.
  • Proof: client testimonials, Google reviews, and photos of real work.
  • A short form: name, phone and what they need. Not ten fields.
  • Availability: when you answer, and what to do in an emergency.

Google: your Business Profile and a page per service

A local tradesperson is found on Google in two ways: in the regular results and on Maps. For Maps you need a Google Business Profile with a service area, hours and reviews, linked to your website. The website strengthens the profile, and the profile brings people to the website. With a multi-page website, you can also build a page for each main service, because someone searching for "electrical panel replacement" is looking for something more specific than "electrician".

What to prepare

  • A list of your services, with a sentence on what each includes.
  • Your service area: cities or neighborhoods.
  • Photos of your work, before and after if you have them.
  • 2 or 3 client testimonials, with their permission.
  • A license, certification or experience worth showing.

Common mistakes on tradespeople's websites

  • A phone number you can't tap on a phone, or one that only appears at the bottom of the page.
  • No service area: inquiries from cities you don't cover, and wasted time on both sides.
  • Anonymous testimonials, or "a happy customer". A testimonial with a first name and city, with the client's permission, is far more convincing.
  • A site that hasn't been updated in years: old prices, or services you no longer offer.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website for a tradesperson cost?

A landing page from ₪2,000, and a multi-page website from ₪4,000. Prices are final, no VAT is added, and they include the first year of domain, hosting and business email.

Do I need a website if I have a Google Business Profile?

The profile is very important, but limited. On a website you explain each service, show work and prices, and get organized inquiries. They work best together.

How do I get more calls from my website?

A call button that is always visible on a phone, a clear service area, real testimonials and a short form. And a fast site, because a slow site on a phone loses people before they even see the button.

Can I add WhatsApp?

Yes. A WhatsApp button with a ready message, next to the call button and the form.

I work in several cities. Does that matter?

It's worth listing them clearly on the site and in your Google Business Profile. With a multi-page website, you can add a page for your main areas, at ₪350 per page beyond 6.

How long does it take?

A landing page up to 4 business days, and a multi-page website up to 10, from the day I have all the materials.
