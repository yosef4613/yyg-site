Landing page design that brings in customers

One focused, fast page where everything leads to a single action: calling you or leaving their details.

Who a landing page is for

  • A business with one main service or a specific promotion.
  • Anyone advertising with a flyer, an ad or on Google who wants everyone who arrives to do one thing.
  • A new business that wants to get started fast on a small budget, and expand to a full website later.

What makes a good landing page

  • One clear headline: who it's for, and what they get.
  • Proof: your work, testimonials or numbers, before you ask for anything.
  • One button that stands out, and appears again at the end of the page.
  • Speed on a phone: most visitors are on their phones, and every second of waiting costs you customers.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a landing page?

Up to 4 business days from the day I receive all the materials, not counting days spent waiting for your reply.

Can I expand the landing page into a full website later?

Yes. The landing page becomes the home page or one of the pages of the website, so we don't start from scratch.

Does a landing page show up on Google?

Yes. It's built ready for Google, but it usually shows up for fewer searches than a full website, because it has fewer pages.

What do I need to prepare?

Your logo, a sentence about your business, the service you want to promote, and photos if you have them. I write the full copy.
