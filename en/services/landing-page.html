Who a landing page is for

  • A business with one main service or a specific promotion.
  • Anyone advertising with a flyer, an ad or on Google who wants everyone who arrives to do one thing.

What makes a good landing page

  • One clear headline: who it's for, and what they get.
  • Proof: your work, testimonials or numbers, before you ask for anything.
  • One button that stands out, and appears again at the end of the page.
  • Speed on a phone: most visitors are on their phones, and every second of waiting costs you customers.

Common landing page mistakes

  • Too many buttons: "call", "WhatsApp", "sign up" and "follow us" on the same screen. With many options, many people choose none. A good page has one main action.
  • A headline about the business, not the customer: "Welcome" instead of what the customer gets and why to choose you.
  • A long form: every unnecessary field costs inquiries. For most businesses, a name and a phone number are enough to call back.
  • A heavy page: a huge video or dozens of photos that slow down loading on a phone, exactly where most visitors are.
  • No proof: promises without work, testimonials or numbers. A visitor who doesn't know you needs a reason to believe.
