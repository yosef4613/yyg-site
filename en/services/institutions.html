Website for institutions, nonprofits and classes

A website that showcases your activities and takes organized sign-ups: parents and participants find what suits them and sign up right from their phones.

What an institution's website needs

  • Finding things fast: a class, a lesson or an activity by age, location or topic.
  • Easy sign-up: a full registration form, with email confirmation.
  • Keeping everyone updated: an opt-in mailing list and ready-made email templates.
  • Taking donations: by card, one-time or monthly, and sometimes a campaign with a goal.
  • Content and images suited to your community, and a clean site that's easy to approve on filtered internet.

What's included

  • Start with 30% and get 3 complete design directions, with your own content
  • 3 revision rounds
  • All the copy: written with the help of AI, personally edited by me and approved by you
  • Fully mobile-friendly and fast to load
  • Full Google readiness: titles, descriptions, a sitemap, structured data and registering the site in Google's tools
  • Accessibility adjustments and an accessibility statement
  • Domain, hosting and business email for the first year. The domain is registered in your name
  • 4 months of support after launch

The base is a business website: up to 6 pages, an update screen for the content that changes, forms and training. An institution with a lot of content chooses an extended business website: up to 12 pages and a content section for news, articles or events. Sign-ups, schedules, a mailing list, user accounts and donations are modules: you add only what your institution needs. Delivery time: up to 14 business days for a business website, and up to 20 for an extended one, plus 2 to 4 days per module.

What good sign-up looks like

  • Find first, then sign up: a parent chooses an age and a city, and sees only what fits. At Pirchei Shomayim, choosing a class fills in the branch on the form automatically.
  • A form that's as short as possible: at first, ask only for what you need to get back to people, and complete the rest in a call.
  • Email confirmation: whoever signs up knows their request arrived, and the office gets an organized request with all the details.
  • Simple management: a screen with all the sign-ups, and Excel export in one click.

A mailing list, done right

The mailing list is built with explicit consent: people who sign up check that they want updates, and every email has an unsubscribe link. That way you only send to people who want to hear from you, which is also what Israeli law requires for promotional email. The mailing list module (₪900) includes 3 ready email templates, for example a new class opening, an event reminder and a general update, and you send them yourself.

Donations and campaigns

With the donations module (₪1,200), people donate by card, one-time or monthly, through a payment provider. Its fees are paid to it directly. The campaign module (₪800) adds a goal meter that shows how much has already been raised, and a donor list. That way everyone who visits sees the campaign is live, and joins in.
