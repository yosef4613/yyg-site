Website for institutions, nonprofits and classes

A website that showcases your activities and takes organized sign-ups: parents and participants find what suits them and sign up right from their phones.

What an institution's website needs

  • Finding things fast: a class, a lesson or an activity by age, location or topic.
  • Easy sign-up: a full registration form, with email confirmation.
  • Keeping everyone updated: an opt-in mailing list and ready-made email templates.
  • Content and images suited to your community, and a clean site that's easy to approve on filtered internet.

What's included

  • Start with 10% and get 3 complete design directions, with your own content
  • 3 revision rounds
  • All the copy: written with the help of AI, personally edited by me and approved by you
  • Fully mobile-friendly and fast to load
  • Full Google readiness: titles, descriptions, a sitemap, structured data and registering the site in Google's tools
  • Accessibility adjustments and an accessibility statement
  • Domain, hosting and business email for the first year. The domain is registered in your name
  • 4 months of support after launch

Up to 8 pages, sign-ups, user accounts, a mailing list and 3 email templates. An admin panel for content, inquiries and subscribers. Delivery time: up to 15 business days.

How it works

  1. A short call: what your business does, who it serves and what the website needs to achieve. Then you get a formal quote.
  2. Start with 10%: and get 3 designs with your own content. You scroll through them on your phone, just like a real website.
  3. You choose a direction: you pay another 40%, and we move on to the build.
  4. Build and review: you review and approve everything, and pay the remaining 50% before the site goes live.
  5. Launch and training, plus 4 months of support.

What good sign-up looks like

  • Find first, then sign up: a parent chooses an age and a city, and sees only what fits. At Pirchei Shomayim, choosing a class fills in the branch on the form automatically.
  • A form that's as short as possible: at first, ask only for what you need to get back to people, and complete the rest in a call.
  • Email confirmation: whoever signs up knows their request arrived, and the office gets an organized request with all the details.
  • Simple management: an admin area with all the requests and their status, and the subscriber list.

A mailing list, done right

The mailing list is built with explicit consent: people who sign up check that they want updates, and every email has an unsubscribe link. That way you only send to people who want to hear from you, which is also what Israeli law requires for promotional email. 3 ready email templates are included, for example a new class opening, an event reminder and a general update, and you send them yourself from the admin area.

  • The number of branches or classes, and whether you need search by age, city or topic.
  • Online payment: connecting a payment provider, at an extra cost shown in the quote.
  • A second language: ₪1,500.

Timeline and what I need from you
