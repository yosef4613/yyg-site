From a simple logo for a new business to a complete brand: logo, colors, fonts, website and content, all in one visual language.

Logo, branding or a full brand: what's the difference?

A logo is your business's mark. Branding is how your business looks everywhere: logo, colors, fonts and a business card. A full brand is everything ready to go: branding, website, content, print, social media and Google, and all you do is approve.

Why it pays to start with the logo

The website is designed around the logo: the colors, the fonts and the feel all grow out of it. When you start with the branding, your website, business card and sign look like one business, not a collection of unrelated pieces.

How it works

  1. A discovery call: what your business does, who it serves, what sets you apart, and which colors and styles you like.
  2. Sketches: one direction for a basic logo, 3 directions for a professional logo and for branding.
  3. Choosing and revisions, and then files ready for every use.

What you get at the end: the files

  • Vector (SVG or PDF): for signs, print and any size, without the logo breaking into pixels.
  • Transparent background (PNG): for the website, presentations and documents.
  • One color: for stamps, engraving and black-and-white printing.
  • An icon: for profile pictures, the site icon and WhatsApp Business.

With the basic logo you get files for the website and social media. With the professional logo and branding you get every type.

What makes a good logo for a small business

  • Simple: readable even at icon size on a phone.
  • Works in one color: a logo that only looks good in color suffers in cheap printing or on a stamp.
  • Fits the audience: a kindergarten logo and an accounting firm logo shouldn't feel the same.
  • Doesn't lean on a trend: effects that look current today get dated fast.

Brand from 0 to 100%: what it means in practice

You bring the idea and the name of the business, and I build everything else in one language: logo and branding, a landing page or a website, all the copy, a business card and flyer, social media images, and a Google Business Profile. For a full brand, payment works in stages like a website: 10% to start, 40% after you choose a direction, and 50% at the end.

How to prepare for the discovery call

  • One sentence that explains what the business does, and for whom.
  • Three words that describe the feeling you want: for example warm, professional, young.
  • Logos you like, and ones you don't, and why.
  • Where the logo will appear: website, sign, vehicle, packaging, stickers or documents.

You don't need to come with a ready idea. That's what sketches are for. The timeline mainly depends on how quickly answers come back between sketches and revisions, and the exact schedule appears in the quote.
