Accessibility Statement

It matters to me that everyone can use this site comfortably, including people with disabilities. The site was built to the WCAG 2.0 guidelines at Level AA, on which Israeli Standard 5568 is based.

What has been done on this site

  • A clear heading structure, with the page language and reading direction marked up.
  • Full keyboard navigation, including in all the interactive tools on the site, with a clear indicator on the item in focus.
  • A "Skip to content" link at the top of the page.
  • Alternative text for images that convey information.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background.
  • An "Animations" toggle that turns off all animations. If you have turned on "reduce motion" in your operating system, the site loads with motion already turned off.
  • Support for phones and for enlarged text.

Known limitations

Some links lead to client websites and to design samples hosted on external sites. These may not be fully accessible.
