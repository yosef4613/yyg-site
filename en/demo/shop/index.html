An online spice and tea store, with a cart, shipping and stock management

A fictional family spice shop called "Gargar", the third generation grinding near the market in Jerusalem. They wanted to sell online and ship across Israel, with about 40 products, free shipping over 250 ₪ and pickup from the shop. We went through the whole process: a questionnaire, a quote, 3 designs, feedback, the final version and the admin screen.

A fictional example store: the name, details and testimonials are made up, and no order is shipped or charged. The images are 3D renders I built specially, and the logo was designed for the example too.

The discovery questionnaire

The shop owner filled in the questionnaire in about 10 minutes and attached his existing logo. These are the answers that shaped the work most:

The business

A family spice and tea shop in Jerusalem. We grind our own spices and blends, sell teas and herbal infusions, and put together gift boxes for the holidays. Now we want to sell online too and ship across Israel.

What sets them apart

We're the third generation grinding in the shop. We grind twice a week in small batches, and every bag shows its grinding date. The blends are my grandfather's recipes, and every product is kosher.

The customers

Private customers, business owners, parents, older people, a religious community. Fresh, fragrant spices for everyday and Shabbat cooking, and a beautiful, ready-made gift for the holidays. Many customers have moved out of Jerusalem and keep ordering from us by phone.

What customers ask most

How much shipping costs and how many days it takes, whether the spice is really fresh, whether there's a kashrut certificate, and whether a greeting card can be added to a gift box.

What he wants to manage himself

Stock and prices, a product that's sold out, new products for the holidays, and tracking orders: what's paid, what's packed and what's shipped. I want to do it myself, from the computer in the shop.

Shipping and stock

We have about 40 products. Most important to me: that I can update stock and prices myself, and that orders arrive organized, with a shipping address. Free shipping over 250 ₪, otherwise 30 ₪, with delivery in 2 to 4 working days. Pickup from the shop is also possible.

The final version

The client chose design 1, "The Spice Market", and paid 40%. I built the store on design 1, with what he asked for from the other designs: the big free-shipping bar from design 3 and the receipt-style cart summary from design 2, both redrawn in the market's language. I also added what he felt was missing: the latest grinding date on every product. From here he approves, pays the final 50%, and the store goes live with real card payments. From that day on, he manages orders, stock, prices and grinding dates himself.

Example

What changed after the feedback

  • A big free-shipping bar under the menu that stays visible while scrolling and shows how much is left. The idea from design 3, in the market's colors and type
  • A receipt-style cart summary: dotted leaders, a double rule before the total, and torn edges. The idea from design 2
  • On the phone the price tags are straight, without the tilt, so the price is easy to read
  • The latest grinding date on every product, like a date stamp: "ground yesterday", "ground on 25.11". Teas and gift boxes: "packed"
  • The small red strip above the menu was removed, since the new bar replaces it
  • The store updates instantly from the admin screen: prices, stock, "sold out", the notice at the top and the grinding date

The store admin screen

This is why we chose an online store: Netanel wants to manage what changes every day himself, from the shop computer or his phone. On this screen he sees a new order the moment it comes in, updates its status, changes prices and stock, and presses "ground today" after every grinding. The store updates right away.

  • Orders: a new order shows up right away, and the status changes with one tap: paid, packed, shipped or picked up
  • Price and stock for every product, and "sold out" with one tap
  • "Ground today" for one product or for all the spices at once, and the date shows in the store
  • A new product with a photo from the phone, resized automatically
  • The free-shipping threshold, the shipping fee and a notice at the top of the store, for example before Purim
