A basic landing page for a handyman, with one design and one revision round

The simplest example, on the basic package: a fictional handyman from Beit Shemesh named Yosef, "Bayit Beseder", who wanted the cheapest option that still looks professional. He brought his own texts, got one design, asked for a few small changes in his one revision round, and the page is ready. We went all the way as with a real client: a questionnaire, a quote, the design, the feedback and the final version.

A fictional example business: the name, details and testimonials are made up. The images are 3D renders I built specially, in place of the client's phone photos. The basic page has no logo design, so the name is simply set in a font.

The discovery questionnaire

Yosef filled in the questionnaire in about 10 minutes, and pasted in the texts he wrote himself: a few sentences about him, and the list of services with prices. He had a few phone photos of his work, and a photo of a magnet with the name. These are the answers that shaped the work most:

The business

A handyman for small home repairs: I put up shelves, curtain rails and lamps, fix doors, handles and taps, assemble furniture, and do small sealing jobs in the bathroom and kitchen.

What sets him apart

I arrive at the time we set, give the price before I start, and leave the place clean. I don't turn down even a 15-minute job, like a single shelf.

What customers ask most

How much it costs, when I can come, whether I bring the screws and wall plugs, and whether I come for one small job too.

How he'll know the page works

3 or 4 more calls a week from people who don't know me, and a proper link I can send in the groups instead of explaining every time what I do.

What he asked for

I want the cheapest option that still looks professional. I wrote the texts myself, and they can be tidied up a little.

What he doesn't want to see

A busy site, with lots of text and photos of people. And it shouldn't look like a big renovation company, because I do small jobs.

The final version

Yosef approved the design, and I changed exactly what he asked for in the one round. From here he checks and approves it, pays the final 50%, and the page goes live. The whole job took 4 business days, from the day the texts and photos arrived.

Example

What changed after the feedback

  • A bigger call button at the top, with "call Yosef" and the number in large type
  • On the phone: a call and WhatsApp bar that always stays at the bottom of the screen
  • Ramat Beit Shemesh added to the service areas, and to the form too
  • The price list is gone. In its place: "How much does it cost?", with a button to send a photo on WhatsApp
  • A toolbox at the top instead of the drill, which looked to him like big renovations
