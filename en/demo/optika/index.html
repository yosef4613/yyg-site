A landing page for an eyewear shop, with hours the owner updates himself

Another complete example, this time a landing page: a fictional eyewear shop called "Tzalul", whose owner asked for one thing above all: to update the opening hours himself, from his phone. We went through the whole process as with a real client: a questionnaire, a quote, 3 designs, the update screen, and feedback.

A fictional example business: the name, details and testimonials are made up. The images are 3D renders I built specially, and the logo was designed for the example too.

The discovery questionnaire

The shop owner filled in the questionnaire in about 10 minutes. His old logo wasn't working for him, and there were no photos at all. These are the answers that shaped the work most:

The business

A neighborhood eyewear shop: eye exams, prescription glasses and sunglasses, contact lenses and repairs.

What sets them apart

Personal attention with no pressure, glasses ready within 3 working days, and free repairs even for glasses bought elsewhere.

The customers

Private customers, parents, older people, a religious community. To see well and look good, at a fair price and without wasting time. Many families with several children who need glasses.

What customers ask most

What the hours are today, whether an exam needs an appointment, how long glasses take, and whether we work with their health fund.

What he wants to update himself

Opening and closing hours, which change for holidays and in winter, and sometimes a note about a sale. I want to update them myself from my phone.

The screen the owner asked for

A standard landing page has no admin area, so I built one small update screen for exactly what he needs. Mark which days are open, change hours, add a holiday notice or a promotion, and save. The page updates right away.

  • Days and hours, including Saturday night
  • A temporary notice with an end date, e.g. before a holiday
  • A live status on the page: "open now", "closes in 25 minutes", "opens tomorrow at 10:00"
  • A promotion on the page, with an end date
  • A check that the hours make sense before saving
  • Easy on the phone: half a minute, no waiting for anyone
Try the update screen The screen is in Hebrew. In the example, changes are saved only in your browser, so anyone can try. Open the final version in another tab and watch it update.
