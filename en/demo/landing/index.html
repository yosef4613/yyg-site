A landing page for a tax-refund advisor, on the standard package with no add-ons

An example of the most common package: a ₪2,000 landing page, with nothing added. A fictional tax-refund advisor called "Nekuda", who works alone from Elad, asked for one page that explains to families why they may be owed money, and brings them to an eligibility check. We went through the whole process as with a real client: a questionnaire, a quote, 3 designs, feedback and the final version.

A fictional example business: the name, details, amounts and testimonials are made up, and the tax-refund information is general only. The images are 3D renders I built specially, and the logo was designed for the example too.

The discovery questionnaire

The advisor filled in the questionnaire in about 10 minutes. He had a clean logo file, no text, and didn't want photos at all. These are the answers that shaped the work most:

The business

I check whether salaried employees and families are owed a tax refund for the last 6 years, and file the request for them. It's all done by phone and email, and I'm only paid if there's a refund.

What sets him apart

I work alone, so you always talk to me, not to a call center. You only pay if there's a refund, and only after the money is in your account. And I know large families well: donations, lots of children, and jobs in several places.

The customers

Private customers, parents, a religious community. To get back money they overpaid, without dealing with forms or leaving home. Many are families with 5 or more children who give a lot to charity and don't know they're owed a tax credit for it.

What customers ask most

Whether they're owed anything at all, what it costs, which documents are needed, how long until the money arrives, and whether it's safe to send documents on WhatsApp.

The goal of the page

Leave details in a form. About 30 eligibility requests a month, and people coming to the call already understanding how it works and what it costs.

Sensitivities

No pictures of people, and modest, respectful language. No exaggerated promises like "get thousands back". I want people to feel they can trust me.

3 words the page should convey

Professional and trustworthy, warm and personal, calm and clean. Background: Light.
