Privacy policy

yygweb.com belongs to YYG Web Design, Yehuda Gordon, web design and development. This page explains in plain words what information the site collects, what it is used for, and what your rights are.

What is collected when you leave your details

  • Your name and email, and a phone number if you choose to add one (optional). In the full form you can also add your business name, a preferred background, sites you like, and notes.
  • Right after you get in touch, one automatic email with the next step is sent to you. When you confirm your email, the confirmation is recorded.
  • In the discovery and design questionnaires: your answers, and any files you attach (for example a logo or photos).
  • If you open a personal area or join the mailing list: your name, email and an encrypted password. You can leave the list at any time with the link in every email.
  • If you sign in with Google: after you approve it, Google sends the site only your name, your email and an account ID number. Your Google password never reaches me. Google's privacy policy also applies to the button itself.
  • If you used the price calculator, chose a package or checked items on the prep list, those choices are attached to your message.

This information is used only to get back to you, prepare a quote, coordinate the work, and, for people on the mailing list, to send new guides. It is not sold, not passed on to advertisers and not used for marketing messages.

Where it is stored

  • Your message goes to my business mailbox at my hosting provider (Hostinger), and a copy reaches my Gmail inbox.
  • A copy is also kept on the site's server, in a folder that cannot be reached from the internet, so that no message gets lost. Only I can see it, on an admin page protected by a key.
  • The site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Visit counting

The site counts visits with a tool I built myself, with no cookies, no Google Analytics and no advertising tools. Only these are kept: which page was viewed, which site you came from (the site name only), and the device type, phone or computer. Your IP address is not stored. Instead, an encrypted code that changes every day is kept, so you cannot be identified or followed from one day to the next.

What is stored in your browser

Your animation preference and the items you check on the "what to prepare" list are stored only in your browser, so you don't have to check them again. They are not sent to me unless you attach them to your message. When you log in to your personal area, a login cookie is stored so you don't have to log in again on every visit.

How long it is kept

Messages are kept for as long as they are needed to handle and manage the work, and for no more than three years after your last message. You can ask for them to be deleted sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information stored about you, correct it or delete it. Call me at 054-854-0700 (calls only) or email me, and I'll handle your request promptly.

Links to other sites

The site links to client websites and to design samples. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
