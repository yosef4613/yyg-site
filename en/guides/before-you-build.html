What to prepare before you build a website

Here are 9 things that get you to a website that fits you perfectly, faster. None of them are required, but each one helps.

Yehuda Gordon, web designer · Updated November 2026

You don’t need to prepare anything to start the conversation. But every item on this list shortens the process, saves revision rounds, and helps us arrive at a website that truly fits you. You can also check off this list on my home page, and it will be attached to your inquiry.

1. Two or three websites you like

This is the single most helpful thing. They don’t have to be in your field. Write one sentence about what you liked: The colors? The layout? The big photos? The clean feel? Even a website you didn’t like helps, because it shows what to avoid.

2. What the website needs to achieve

Should people call you? Leave their details? Sign up for a class? Order a product? Choose one main goal. A website that tries to do everything equally ends up doing nothing well.

3. Who your customers are

Who visits the site, and what do they want to know? Tip: think about the questions people ask you most often on the phone. The answers usually make the best headings for your website.

4. Design preferences

A dark or light background? Colors you love, and colors you don’t? A classic, modern, or festive style? If you write this down in advance, the design directions you receive will be closer to what you imagined, and you won’t have to sift through examples that miss the mark. “I don’t know yet” is a perfectly good answer too: in that case, I’ll make the design directions clearly different from one another.

5. A logo

Your logo is the foundation of the design: the site’s colors, fonts, and overall feel all grow out of it. Send it in the best quality you have, ideally the original file from your designer. Don’t have a logo yet? It’s best to create a logo and branding first, then build the website around them. I also design logos: a basic logo for ₪590, or a professional logo with 3 concepts for ₪1,500. A basic brand identity with colors, fonts and a business card is ₪2,500.

6. Real photos

Photos of your work, your products, your place, or your team. A good phone photo taken in daylight beats a stock photo, because customers can tell right away what’s real. If you don’t have photos, we can start with a few and add more later.

7. Your content, in bullet points

What you offer, what it costs (if you want to publish prices), opening hours, service area, and common questions. Bullet points are enough. I write the full copy with the help of AI and edit it personally, and you approve it.

8. A web address, if you have one

If you already have a domain, let me know where it’s registered. Don’t have one yet? I’ll help you choose a name that’s short, easy to spell over the phone, and easy to remember.

9. Timeline and budget

Is there a date the site has to be live by? A launch, a season, an event? Write it down, and we’ll plan backward from it. Share a rough budget range too, so I can recommend the right package and nothing more than you need.

How to reach out so you get exactly what you need

  • One sentence about your business: what you do and for whom, as if you were explaining it to a neighbor.
  • Links to websites you liked: there’s a field for them in the form.
  • What doesn’t work for you: a color, a style, or anything that doesn’t fit your audience. This saves a revision round.
  • Not sure about something? That’s fine. That’s what the call is for, and I’ll ask the questions.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to prepare the whole list before I reach out?

No. You can reach out without preparing anything at all. Each item on the list simply makes the process shorter.

I don’t have a logo. Can you still build my website?

It’s best to start with the logo, because the website’s design is built around it. If you don’t have one, I can create a basic logo (₪590), a professional logo (₪1,500) or a basic brand identity (₪2,500) before the website.

Who writes the website copy?

You send me the information in bullet points, and I write all the copy with the help of AI and edit it personally. You approve every word before the site goes live.

Want to know what your website will cost?

The discovery questionnaire takes about 10 minutes, and at the end you see a price estimate from your answers right away. And if we’re a good fit, we start with 10%, and I’ll prepare 3 design directions for your website before you go ahead.
