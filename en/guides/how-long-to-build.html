How long does it take to build a business website?

Real delivery times, the stages, and what usually slows things down. Most delays have nothing to do with the build itself.

Yehuda Gordon, web designer · Updated November 2026

The short answer

The build itself takes days, not months. These are my delivery times, in business days, from the day I have all the materials:

Type of siteDelivery timeStarting price
Landing pageUp to 4 business days₪2,000
Website with admin panelUp to 10 business days₪4,000
Website with sign-ups and a mailing listUp to 15 business days₪6,000
Online storeUp to 20 business days₪7,000

Prices are final, and no VAT is added. Days spent waiting for your reply aren't counted. So what really decides how long it takes is mostly how quickly the materials and approvals come in.

The stages, one after another

  1. A call and a quote: what the business needs, and which package fits.
  2. Materials: logo, key points for the copy, photos, and sites you like.
  3. 3 designs with your own content, and choosing a direction.
  4. The build: all the pages, forms and the admin panel.
  5. Revisions and testing on phone and computer.
  6. Connecting the domain, going live, and training.

What slows things down

  • Materials that trickle in: the logo today, photos next week, and text for one page two weeks later.
  • Copy nobody writes: one of the most common delays. With me, I write it, and you just give key points and approve.
  • Too many decision makers: when every change needs three people's approval, each round takes longer. It's worth choosing one person to approve.
  • Changing direction midway: after you've chosen a design and the build has started, changing the whole structure sets the process back.
  • For a store: payment provider approval, which takes a few days, and a product list that isn't ready yet.
  • A domain stuck with a previous provider: transferring a domain can take a few days, and sometimes needs a transfer code from the old provider.

How to speed things up

  • Prepare the list from the guide what to prepare before you build a website before the first call.
  • Send all the materials at once, even if they're not perfect.
  • Reply to each round within a day or two.
  • If there's a deadline, like a business opening or an event, mention it in the first call.
  • For a store: open an account with a payment provider on day one.

What gets checked before launch

  • Every form sends, and inquiries reach the right inbox.
  • The site opens fast on a phone, and every button works with a finger.
  • The copy is approved, with no mistakes in phone numbers, addresses or prices.
  • The domain is connected, and the site runs on a secure connection.
  • There's an accessibility statement and a privacy policy.

After launch, 4 months of support begin: bug fixes, questions, and small changes.

What you can do while I build

  • Open a Google Business Profile, so you can link it to the website on launch day.
  • Ask 2 or 3 happy clients for testimonials, with their permission, so they're on the site from day one.
  • Make a list of who to tell when the site goes live: existing clients, groups and suppliers.
  • Plan the next step: a flyer with a QR code, an ad, or a link in your email signature.

And what about Google?

A new website doesn't appear on Google the day it goes live. Google needs to find it, crawl it and understand what it's about. On launch day I register the site in Google's tools and submit a sitemap, which shortens that time. Showing up in the results usually takes days to weeks, and climbing in the rankings takes longer and depends on content, competition and reviews. That's why it's worth opening a Google Business Profile and asking happy clients for reviews.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a landing page?

With me, up to 4 business days from the day I have all the materials.

Can a website be built in a week?

A landing page, yes. A multi-page website takes me up to 10 business days, about two weeks, if the materials and replies come quickly.

From which day is the delivery time counted?

From the day I have all the materials: logo, key points for the copy and photos. Days spent waiting for your reply aren't counted.

When will the website show up on Google?

On launch day the site is registered in Google’s tools. Showing up in the results usually takes days to weeks, and rankings improve over time with good content.

What delays a website build the most?

Usually not the build itself, but materials that come in slowly, copy nobody writes, and approvals from several people.
