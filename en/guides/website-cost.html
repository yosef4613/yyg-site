How much does a business website cost?

Real prices, what drives them, and how to see the design before you commit to the full price.

Yehuda Gordon, web designer · Updated November 2026

The short answer

The price of a website depends mainly on three things: how many pages it has, what the site needs to do (just present information, take sign-ups, or sell), and who updates it afterward. Here are my starting prices, so you have a reference point. No VAT is added. What makes a website more expensive, and what doesn’t

1. The number of pages

Every page needs design, content, and testing on a phone. A landing page is one long page. A typical business website usually has 4 to 8 pages: home, services, about, FAQ, and contact.

2. What the site does

A site that only presents information is the least expensive. As soon as it needs to take sign-ups, send emails, manage user accounts, or accept payments, there’s more to build and more to test.

3. Who updates the site

If your content hardly ever changes, you can skip the update screen and save. If you post news, promotions, products, or schedules, an update screen is worth it: you make the updates yourself, without waiting on anyone.

4. Custom design or a template

A ready-made template costs less at first, but it looks like hundreds of other websites. A custom design is built around your business, your customers, and the action you want them to take.

5. Accessibility

Israeli accessibility regulations require many businesses and institutions to make their websites accessible. Building accessibility in from the start costs far less than retrofitting an existing site. I include it in every package.

Ongoing costs once your site is ready

  • Domain (your website’s address): a .com address usually costs a few dozen shekels a year.
  • Hosting (the server your site lives on): usually a few hundred shekels a year, depending on the provider and the plan.
  • Business email on your domain: optional. Some hosting providers include it free with the plan.

I include the first year of domain, hosting, and business email in the price of the website. From the second year on, you choose: All-in Care, where I take care of everything for ₪590 a year (₪890 for a store), or a move to a hosting provider of your own. If you’d like me to make small changes for you, you can add maintenance of up to half an hour a month, for ₪199 a month. Either way, the domain is registered in your name, so the website is truly yours.

How to avoid committing to a website you haven’t seen

The biggest risk in building a website is paying a lot up front and finding out at the end that the design isn’t what you had in mind. With me, you pay in stages, and you see the design before you go ahead:

  1. After a short call and a formal quote, you pay 30% to start, and for that you already get 3 complete design directions for your website, using your own content.
  2. Love one of the directions? You pay another 40%, and we move on to the build. And if you decide not to go ahead, the 30% covers the design work, and you don’t pay anything more.
  3. You pay the remaining 30% at the end, after you’ve reviewed and approved everything, before the site goes live.

With a basic landing page, which has one design, you pay 50% at the start and 50% before the site goes live.
