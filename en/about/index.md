# About: YYG Web Design

Nice to meet you, I'm Yehuda Gordon. I design and build websites for small businesses and institutions. I do all the work myself, from the first sketch to launch, so you always talk to the same person.

## How I work

- We start with a short questionnaire or a call, and you get a formal, fixed quote.
- You pay 10% and get 3 complete designs of your own site, with your content. You choose with your eyes, not your imagination.
- You pick a design, pay another 40%, and I build. You pay the final 50% only after you confirm everything is right.
- No VAT is added to the prices.

## What matters to me in a website

- That it brings in customers: clear about what you do, and clear about the next step.
- That it loads fast, even on a phone with a weak signal.
- That it is accessible, ready for Google and AI tools, and that you can update what changes.
