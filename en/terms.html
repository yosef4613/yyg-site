Terms of service

Prices

  • Prices on this site are starting prices, an estimate only. The calculator and the questionnaire also give a first estimate.
  • The exact price is set by your needs, and appears in a formal quote by email before we start.
  • No VAT is added to the prices.
  • Launch price: 10% off for the next 5 clients, in return for showing the site in my work with a written testimonial.

Getting started

  • We start with a short questionnaire or a call, and then you get a formal quote.
  • Work starts after you approve the quote in writing (on the quote page or by email) and make the first payment.
  • What each package includes, how many pages, designs and revision rounds, is written in the quote.

Payments

  • 30% to start, and you get 3 complete designs of your site. 40% after you choose a design, before the build. 30% at the end, after your approval and before going live.
  • For the basic landing page: 50% to start and 50% before going live.
  • The first 30% is part of the price, not an extra. If you decide not to go ahead after the designs, it covers the design work, and you pay nothing more.
  • Each payment is due within 7 days of the request.
  • Payments made are not refunded, and a stage that has started is paid for the work done.

Timing and working together

  • Delivery times are in business days. They are counted from the day I receive all the materials (text, photos and logo), not including days spent waiting for your reply.
  • A stage with no reply within 10 business days counts as approved.
  • If the text, photos and logo don't arrive within 30 days, the project is paused and goes back in the queue.
  • A revision round is one consolidated list of comments, not a separate message for each change.
  • Any work not listed in the quote is priced in advance, and done only with your approval.

Ownership

  • The domain is in your name from the start.
  • The site and content are yours with the last payment.
  • The designs you didn't choose stay mine.
  • The text, photos and logo you send should be yours, or ones you are allowed to use.

Support and care

  • After launch you get support: 4 months in every package, and one month for the basic landing page.
  • From the second year you can continue with "All-in Care": domain, hosting, business email, security, backups and fixes, for ₪590 a year, or ₪890 a year for a store.
  • Maintenance, updates and small changes up to half an hour a month: ₪199 a month. Optional.
